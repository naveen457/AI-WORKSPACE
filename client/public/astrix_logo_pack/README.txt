ASTRIX LOGO PACK
================

Brand direction
- Hybrid four-point star + subtle agent orbit
- Midnight navy: #0B0D1A
- Electric violet: #8B5CF6 / #A855F7
- Icy cyan: #67E8F9
- Soft white: #F7F8FF

Folders
- svg/: primary dark/light lockups, icon-only marks, monochrome variants
- png/: raster exports where generated
- favicon/: SVG favicon and multiple PNG sizes

Recommended usage
- Website header: svg/astrix-primary-dark.svg on dark backgrounds
- Light pages: svg/astrix-primary-light.svg
- App avatar: svg/astrix-icon-dark.svg or svg/astrix-icon-light.svg
- Browser favicon: favicon/favicon.svg (use favicon-32.png or favicon-48.png where available)
- Monochrome variants: use for one-color print or constrained UI contexts

Notes
- SVG is the master format and scales without losing quality.
- Review typography in your own brand environment before launch; system font rendering may vary.
- The favicon is simplified for legibility at small sizes.
